# Privacy Policy for Vesper Pop

_Last updated: July 13, 2026_

Vesper Pop ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy completely.

## Data we collect

**None.**

The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has:

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics or tracking.
- No advertising.
- No network connections of any kind.

Everything the app does happens entirely on your device.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, no advertising networks, and no analytics providers. No data is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

## Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL.
