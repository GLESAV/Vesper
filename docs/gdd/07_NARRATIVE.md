# Vesper — Narrative & Writing Bible

*Vesper has no plot, but it has a world and a voice — and for this audience the
voice is a core feature, not garnish.*

## 1. The world (canon)

The game takes place in **the held evening** — the moment after sunset,
lasting as long as you need it to. Canon objects: the field (the air where
worries drift as orbs), the sky (where your path is written in first stars),
the journal (a kept book on a windowsill), the lamp (lit when you visit),
fortunes (small truths orbs were carrying), Vesper itself — the evening star —
and its other name, **Morning Star** (pop #100): the promise that the evening
always ends kindly.

Nothing in canon is mystical or religious; it is domestic and cosmic at once —
a kitchen-window view of the universe.

## 2. The voice

**A wise, warm friend at dusk.** Lowercase-calm, brief, kind, a little wry,
never saccharine, never instructive, never exclamatory.

| Rule | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Address gently, rarely | "you're allowed to enjoy something this small." | "You did it!!! 🎉" |
| Observe, don't command | "the sky noticed." | "Check out the Sky Map!" |
| Kind, not clinical | "set free" | "DETONATED", "destroyed", "cleared: 12" |
| Wry is welcome | "you can always make more worries. try not to." | sarcasm at the player's expense |
| Never urgency | "again?" | "Don't stop now!" |
| Never loss-framing | "the road behind folded itself away." | "Your path expired." |

Forbidden vocabulary anywhere in the product: streak, fail, miss, expire,
lose/lost, blast, destroy, kill, score (as a noun), limited, hurry, last chance.

## 3. Fortunes (the flagship writing)

The 18 shipped fortunes set the register. Writing rules for new ones:
one sentence · ≤ 12 words ideally · concrete over abstract · lands like a
friend's aside, not a fortune cookie · no imperatives ("breathe" earns its one
exception) · reward re-reading. The Journal archives every fortune found —
they are collectible writing, treat each like a tiny product.

Growth plan: +18 per arrival (03 §Content), themed subtly by phase of the
evening; secret pops carry one unique fortune each.

## 4. Naming

- Pops: short, concrete, dusk-adjacent nouns (existing catalog is canon).
- Places: always lowercase in-world: `the sky`, `the field`, `your journal`.
- Features never get marketing names in-product (no "Pop Pass", ever).

## 5. String inventory & process

Every user-facing string lives through the voice test: read it aloud at 1 a.m.
in an empty kitchen — if it sounds like software, rewrite it. The v2 warmth
pass (04 §4 canonical strings) is Phase 0 work and includes the App Store
listing, which already carries the voice well.
