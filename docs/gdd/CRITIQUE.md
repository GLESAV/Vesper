## 3. The evaluation prompt (template instantiated per expert × artifact)

> *[Expert persona — credentials, stance, biases]*
> You are one advisor on a panel critiquing the planning documents for Vesper.
> **Mission: a stress-free, genuinely fun mobile game. Goal: enjoyable.
> Audience: women 25–35.** Read `01_VISION.md` for the adopted vision (context),
> then read and critique **[artifact]** from your domain. Judge against (a) the
> mission and audience, (b) the craft bar of a AAA studio in your discipline,
> (c) internal consistency with sibling docs (readable for context; critique
> only your artifact). Be rigorous, specific, tough-but-fair: one strength,
> then what is weak, missing, wrong, or contradictory. Prefer issues that would
> change what gets built. Every issue carries severity (blocker / major /
> minor) and a concrete, decidable recommendation.

Player advisors receive the same brief re-framed: *you are not a designer;
react as this real player — where would this delight you, where would it lose
you; your recommendations are what would make you actually play.*

## 4. Pipeline

1. **Critique** — 30 dedicated subprocesses (3 per artifact), structured output.
2. **Compile & plan per artifact** — 10 subprocesses: digest the three critiques,
   note expert conflicts, emit a prioritized action plan (≤ 10 actions, P0–P2).
3. **Reconcile** — 1 subprocess: assemble all 10 plans into a master action plan;
   surface cross-document tensions as explicit decisions-needed.
4. **Decide** — the director (main session) reviews the master plan, resolves
   every open decision in service of the mission, adjusts for consistency.
5. **Execute** — apply the decided actions to the documents; append outcomes here.

## 5.2 Adopted decisions (23)

**D1 — Simulation fence vs fun gate.** Keep the fence as the default, make it conditional: the sim stays untouched UNLESS the v1.2 baseline fun gate (M1) fails, in which case a loop-depth workstream opens with the existing one-constant-at-a-time GameConfig discipline. This preserves Hana's discipline while removing the license to ship an un-fun game.

**D2 — Shape of the one success metric.** Wind-down return remains the single release bar (one gate a solo dev can actually decide against); add commuter and collector behavioral criteria as monitored secondary signals that inform iteration but never gate the ship. Record this reasoning in 00 so the tension is closed, not implicit.

**D3 — Measurement instrument for return-rate.** TestFlight aggregate session data as the primary return-rate instrument (SDK-free, guardrail-compliant), diary cohort kept strictly separate for qualitative texture. If TestFlight data proves unusable, fall back to moderated day-7 interviews and delete the 'unprompted' claim rather than pretend to measure it.

**D4 — Phase 0 fallback design.** Sequence them: Jun's tap-labels-primary is fallback stage 1 (least rework, preserves One World), Hana's static-field panels is stage 2 if the camera itself fails perf or motion-sensitivity gates. Write both into 08 so spike failure re-scopes rather than stalls.

**D5 — iOS edge handling and swipe commit numbers.** Dead zones, no deferral — 'her evening, respected' means the OS gesture always works instantly; verify whispers cover the dead regions. For the commit threshold, start the spike at ~10–12% screen height with a velocity gate and let on-device testing pick the final number; the doc records the measured value, not a guess.

**D6 — Exit-test pass bar and panel sourcing.** n≥8 fresh testers, pass at ≥7/8 with no failure sharing a common cause, one fix-and-retest cycle allowed per gate; recruitment shortfall is a schedule risk, never a bar-lowering event. Panel recruited primarily outside the owner's network (friends cannot fail a calm game honestly), existing panel used only for the v1.2 baseline where Hana's pragmatism is fine.

**D7 — Business model: free-as-identity vs premium.** Keep free-as-identity for v2.0 — it is shipped brand and the share-loop math depends on zero friction — but write the honest one-paragraph math Cole demands, and list a dignified paid path (tip jar per June's canon rule, or a 'supporter edition') among the pre-approved sustainability fallbacks with numeric triggers, decided now, not under duress.

**D8 — Ratings ask.** Colin's option (a): a single system-mediated SKStoreReviewController request after the third field clear (OS-throttled, dismissible, not a nag by the guardrail's spirit), plus June's passive link in the journal. Rewrite the KPI to ≥4.7 with a volume floor and drop ≥4.8. The current doc holds a KPI that requires prompting and a ban on prompting — any decision is better than that.

**D9 — The breath: secrecy vs de-earnability.** Adopt all three, they compose: spec the chime as ambient un-earnable variation (satisfies Marta/Aria), never label or explain it anywhere (satisfies Priya), and defer implementation to the v2.1 arrival (satisfies 08's scope math).

**D10 — Pressure-free return mechanism.** Combine with a stated primary: in-fiction nightly variation (one small surprise per ordinary evening) is the core bet — it's the game's job to be worth returning to; context anchoring ships later, optional and off by default. Validate both hypotheses in the 2-week diary before Phase 1 features lock.

**D11 — Casing of fortunes.** Sentence-case fortunes as a stated, canonical exception; lowercase for all UI/whisper copy. This preserves the shipped corpus, gives the two registers a visible signature, and costs nothing — June's consistency concern is answered by making the exception explicit rather than accidental.

**D12 — Fortune canon: worry metaphor and the 'score' line.** Adopt Vale's structure with Cole's gate: canonical metaphor is 'small lights', copy never diagnoses the player's inner state; the forbidden-vocabulary rule bans frames applied to the player (keeping the score fortune as the worked allowed case); then run every fortune through Cole's 'real and serious worry' sensitivity test — it catches the dismissive lines under either metaphor.

**D13 — World-move sounds under Reduce Motion.** Always play under RM by default — accessibility orientation beats preference. Honor Dani with a separate all-users 'transition sounds' toggle in the same surface as sound/haptics; suppression is then the player's explicit choice, never the default, and the RM user who needs the cue keeps it.

**D14 — Localization scope.** English-only v2.0, stated as canon in 07, 08, and 09. Phase 3 delivers only string-catalog extraction plus per-language voice briefs; each translated release ships as its own post-2.0 arrival gated by native-speaker voice review. For a solo dev, five simultaneous transcreations is the single largest hidden scope item in the suite.

**D15 — v2.0 scope of the six added systems.** v2.0 ships arrival, evening light, the lamp, and keepsakes; the breath and the widget move to v2.1 (already 08's position); the share card ships only if it passes the no-numbers constraint. The lamp and keepsakes are cheap relative to their pull for the target audience — Aria's concern is honored by the written cut order (M23) naming them negotiable before the pop's feel budget is ever touched.

**D16 — Count on the share card.** Default is field + fortune only; tonight's count is an explicit per-share opt-in; lifetime totals never appear on shareable surfaces. Priya gets her screenshot by choosing it; the product never pushes a comparable number.

**D17 — Bluetooth audio-haptic de-fusion.** Per route class: wired/speaker stays fused (≤10 ms bound); on Bluetooth, accept de-fusion with the haptic authoritative — delaying touch confirmation by 150–300 ms to chase late audio breaks the one thing that must never lag, the feel of the pop under the finger.

**D18 — Parity backbone: haptics vs visual.** Visual parity is the guaranteed backbone (it is the only channel present on every device in every mode); richer muted-mode haptic phrasing is funded as a named iPhone enhancement with Dani's real-playlist QA pass. Both experts are right about different devices; the doc should say so.

**D19 — Contrast measurement standard.** APCA as the design standard — it is materially more accurate for exactly this product's regime (low-luminance text on near-black OLED) — with a one-time WCAG 4.5:1 spot-check on functional text for conventional compliance reporting. One standard, one tool, named in 05.

**D20 — Do research assumptions gate Phase 0?.** Proceed under tagged assumptions (Sato/Reyes) — a solo production cannot afford a research-blocked critical path — with two exceptions that DO block because they are cheap and load-bearing: the ~90s field-clear measurement and the Phase 0a gesture spike. Every other assumption gets a named check and a written revisit rule.

**D21 — iPad for v2.0.** Option (b): v2.0 is iPhone-first, existing iPad layout grandfathered, Phase 3's 'big sky pass' pretense removed, iPad interaction design revisited as its own v2.x arrival. Adding an iPad spec to Phase 0 threatens the solo-dev critical path for a minority of the target audience's sessions.

**D22 — 'Anxiety' as a signal.** Hana's split: remove 'anxiety' from every owned surface (keywords, subtitle, screenshots, copy) but keep passively counting it in unprompted reviews — observing the players' own words is data, not a claim, and it costs nothing.

**D23 — Content cadence commitment.** The steady-state ritual rests on existing systems (infinite Path + 100-pop catalog + fortune pool) — soften the Monument Valley / Animal Crossing comps cells accordingly. Arrivals are a bonus rhythm: commit publicly only to arrival #1 (built in Phase 3 as the pipeline proof with its cost recorded); the 6–8-week cadence stays internal until three drops have shipped on time.
