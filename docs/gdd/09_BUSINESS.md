# Vesper — Positioning, Pricing & Launch

*The business plan for a game whose brand is "asks nothing." Every commercial
choice must itself be stress-free.*

## 1. Positioning

**The game in the wellness slot; the wellness in the game slot.** (02 §comps)
One line for the store and the pitch: *"the evening you keep in your pocket."*
Category: Games › Casual (primary), Lifestyle (secondary consideration).
Vesper competes with Two Dots on feel, Monument Valley on beauty, and Calm on
the bedside slot — while being the only one that is simultaneously free of
pressure, free of ads, and a real game.

## 2. Pricing decision

**Free. No ads, no IAP, no subscription.** (Confirmed as identity, not
deferral: "no monetization" is the single strongest differentiator with this
audience and this mission; any monetization would need its own pillar review
and is out of scope for v2.)

Considered and rejected for v2: paid up-front (kills the share-loop that this
audience powers); tip jar (even a kind ask is an ask — revisit post-v2 only if
the owner wants one, framed as "leave a lantern", never interrupting).

## 3. Growth model (organic by design)

- **The screenshot loop:** the game is built to be photographed (05); the
  shareable stillness card (03) makes it one gesture. Quiet `vesper` wordmark
  corner credit — discoverable, never watermark-loud.
- **ASO:** name "Vesper Pop"; subtitle "pop orbs, quietly let go" (shipped);
  keyword themes: calm games, cozy, anxiety, satisfying, bubble pop, no ads.
  Screenshots lead with the field + one whisper of copy each (never feature
  bullets); preview video = the 30 s cut (demo/DEMO_SCRIPT.md).
- **Community seeding at launch:** cozy-gaming communities, calm-tech press
  (the "no data, no ads, no pressure" story is genuinely newsworthy), and the
  playtest panel as first advocates.
- **Ratings ask:** none in v2.0. The App Store listing must never be fed by an
  interruption. (Revisit a single, post-third-clear, dismissible-forever ask
  only if organic volume is insufficient — pillar-reviewed.)

## 4. Brand voice outside the app

Same voice as in (07): lowercase-kind, wry, brief. The store listing, the
privacy page (tfc.studio/vesper), release notes ("The Feel & Collection
Update" register), and any social presence all speak it. Privacy is marketed
as a feature, always: **"Vesper collects nothing."**

## 5. Success, measured without surveillance

- App Store: rating ≥ 4.8, reviews mentioning "calm/sleep/anxiety" unprompted.
- Organic: screenshots/videos appearing in cozy-game communities.
- Panel: next-evening unprompted return (the one metric, 03 §6).
- Store analytics only (Apple-provided, aggregate): retention proxy via
  update-adoption curves; no in-app telemetry, ever.

## 6. Launch beats (v2.0)

1. TestFlight panel wave (Phase 2) → testimonials with consent.
2. Press/creator kit: the plan's one-pager, the stillness cards, the preview
   video, the "collects nothing" story.
3. v2.0 "One World" release with refreshed listing.
4. First *arrival* content drop 6–8 weeks later — the second press beat.
