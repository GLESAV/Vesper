The Feel & Collection Update.

Vesper plays exactly the way it did — it just feels better, and there is more of it to find.

- Every pop now lands with a soft haptic tap and instant sound, sized to the orb.
- 100 pops to discover: embers, tides, blooms, frost, chimes, lanterns and more — each with its own colors, debris, sound, and ripple. The original is pop #001, unchanged.
- The Journey: a quiet collection screen with your pop points, records, and what's somewhere ahead. Feature one pop, or drift among everything you've found.
- Pop points only ever add up. Nothing is lost, timed, ranked, or sold. Unlocks are gifts, not gates.
- Little point whispers drift up from each pop (you can turn them off).
- A gentle chime when you clear the field; faint motes drift in the dark.
- Kinder to your battery, your motion settings, and VoiceOver.

Still no ads, no accounts, no timers, and nothing collected. Just open it and breathe.
