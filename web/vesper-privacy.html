Vesper Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 July 2026

Vesper Pop is built to respect your privacy completely.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account or sign-in, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, and makes no network connections of any kind. Everything happens entirely on your device.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics providers. No data is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of any age.
