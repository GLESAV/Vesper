Vesper Pop — Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026

Vesper Pop is built to respect your privacy completely.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. It has no account or sign-in, no analytics or tracking, no advertising, no in-app purchases, no notifications, and it makes no network connections of any kind. Everything happens entirely on your device.

In App Store Connect the app is declared as Data Not Collected, which is the whole of the story.

What the app keeps on your device

So that the world is still there when you come back, the app saves a little state in the ordinary iOS preferences store, on your device only:

  • your settings — sound, haptics, point whispers;
  • your pop points, the pops you have found, and your records;
  • the path you have walked, and the field you were last on.

None of this identifies you, none of it leaves the phone, and none of it is backed by an account. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks, or analytics providers. It has no external dependencies at all — everything in it was written for it. No data is ever sent to us or to anyone else, because there is nothing in the app that can send anything anywhere.

Crash reports

The app itself reports nothing. If you have turned on Apple's own Share With App Developers setting, Apple may send us anonymous, aggregated crash and performance diagnostics, the same way it can for any app. That is Apple's mechanism and Apple's setting; you can turn it off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, there is nothing about a child — or anyone else — for it to gather, keep, or pass on.

Changes
